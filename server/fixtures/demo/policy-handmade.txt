Since every custom piece is made to order, personalized items are non-refundable once production starts. For standard stock items, returns are accepted within 30 days of delivery. Returns requested between 30 and 45 days incur a 15% restocking fee, and anything after 45 days will be rejected. Shipping fees are non-refundable. If something arrives damaged, send us a photo and we'll figure out a fair fix or refund the damaged part.
